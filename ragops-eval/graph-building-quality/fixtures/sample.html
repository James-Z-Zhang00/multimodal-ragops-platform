Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition

On February 1, 2025, Apple Inc. issued a press release regarding Apple's financial results for its fiscal year 2025 first quarter ended December 28, 2024. Apple reported quarterly revenue of $124.3 billion, up 4% year over year. The Company posted quarterly earnings per diluted share of $2.40, up 10% year over year.

Services revenue reached an all-time high of $26.3 billion during the quarter, representing a 14% increase compared to the same quarter last year. iPhone revenue was $69.1 billion, Mac revenue was $8.99 billion, iPad revenue was $8.09 billion, and Wearables, Home and Accessories revenue was $11.75 billion.

Tim Cook, Apple's Chief Executive Officer, said: "We are thrilled to report Apple's best January quarter ever, with revenues up 4 percent to $124 billion, fueled by our record Services business and the strength of our installed base of over 2.35 billion active devices."

Luca Maestri, Apple's Chief Financial Officer, said: "Our earnings per share of $2.40 grew 10 percent year over year, and we returned over $30 billion to shareholders during the quarter."

Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk

Apple is exposed to market risk related to changes in interest rates and foreign currency exchange rates. The Company regularly reviews its exposure to these risks and takes steps to mitigate them as appropriate.

Interest Rate Risk. As of December 28, 2024, the Company had fixed-income securities and cash equivalents with a fair value of $53.8 billion, subject to interest rate risk. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would result in a $1.2 billion decline in the fair value of these securities.

Foreign Currency Risk. Apple operates internationally and is exposed to foreign currency risk. International net sales represented 57% of total net sales for the first quarter of fiscal year 2025. The Company uses foreign currency forward contracts and option contracts to partially offset the foreign exchange risk on expected future cash flows.

Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Appointment of Certain Officers

On January 15, 2025, Apple's Board of Directors appointed Dr. Sarah Chen, age 47, as Executive Vice President and Chief Technology Officer, effective March 1, 2025. Dr. Chen succeeds John Ternus, who will transition to the role of Chief Product Officer.

Dr. Chen previously served as Senior Vice President of Hardware Technologies at Apple since 2019. Her annualized base salary will be $1,200,000. For fiscal year 2025, her target annual incentive opportunity will be 200% of base salary. She will receive an equity award valued at approximately $15,000,000, consisting of 60% performance-based restricted stock units and 40% restricted stock units.

John Ternus will continue as Chief Product Officer with an annualized base salary of $1,000,000 and a target annual incentive of 150% of base salary.
